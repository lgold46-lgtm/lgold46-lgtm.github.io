Our Story

Born from a moment
every parent dreads.

Mason's Little Aid started with a real-life scare — a burn on an outing, no burn shield in sight, and the kind of panic no parent should have to feel. That moment made it clear: parents deserve better than hoping the venue has what they need.

So we built it. A compact, thoughtfully packed first aid kit that fits in your handbag, your baby bag, your backpack — wherever your day takes you and your little ones. No medication, no bulk, no faff. Just the essentials, beautifully put together, right when you need them most.
